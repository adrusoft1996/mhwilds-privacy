Account & data deletion

MH Wilds Build Planner (com.adrusoft.mhwilds.planner)
Last updated: 7 September 2026
Contact: adrisansal.327@gmail.com

This page explains how to request deletion of a Community account associated with MH Wilds Build Planner and what data is removed.

How to request deletion

  1. Email adrisansal.327@gmail.com from the address linked to your Google account (if you linked Google), or include your in-app hunter display name / Community UID if you know it.
  2. Use the subject line: MH Wilds Build Planner — Account deletion request.
  3. We will delete or anonymize Community account data associated with your Firebase Auth user within 30 days of verifying the request.

What is deleted

  • Firebase Authentication account for this app (anonymous and/or linked Google)
  • Hunter profile fields stored for Community (display name, bio, avatar URL references)
  • Published builds, votes, and reports tied to that account (or anonymized where retention is required for abuse prevention)
  • Push notification tokens for that account

What is not deleted automatically

  • Data stored only on your device (saved builds, wishlist, material inventory, Gemini API key): uninstall the app or clear app storage. We cannot access that data remotely.
  • Aggregated or anonymized logs that Google Firebase may retain under Google’s policies.

Español — cómo solicitar el borrado

  1. Escribe a adrisansal.327@gmail.com desde el correo de tu cuenta Google (si la vinculaste), o incluye tu nombre de cazador / UID.
  2. Asunto: MH Wilds Build Planner — Solicitud de borrado de cuenta.
  3. Eliminaremos o anonimizaremos los datos de Comunidad de esa cuenta en un máximo de 30 días.

Los datos solo locales (builds, wishlist, API key) se borran desinstalando la app o limpiando el almacenamiento; no tenemos acceso remoto a ellos.
